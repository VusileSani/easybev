How it works

From QR scan to official bill.

EasyBev sits between the guest and the venue POS. It improves service and order capture without forcing the venue to rebuild its existing systems.

1

Guest connects

The guest scans the waiter's permanent EasyBev QR and joins a live service session.

2

Waiter captures

The waiter enters orders directly into EasyBev instead of relying on handwritten notes.

3

Guest tracks

The guest can call the waiter, send messages and see the running bill update in real time.

4

POS reconciles

At bill time, the waiter sees the full itemised EasyBev list, reconciles it in the POS and issues the official bill.

For venues

Less friction between guest, waiter and POS.

EasyBev is designed around the moments where service usually breaks down: missed calls, forgotten requests, handwritten orders and rushed reconciliation.

01

Digital waiter pad

Orders are captured once in a structured service session instead of scattered across scraps of paper.

02

Guest service channel

Call Waiter and Message actions give guests a direct, low-friction way to get attention.

03

Live running bill

Guests can follow what has been captured and see the running total throughout the session.

04

Manager-controlled menu intelligence

Managers maintain item names and prices so waiter entry can use fast suggestions without turning EasyBev into a menu app.

05

Permanent waiter QR slots

Waiter QR codes remain reusable while managers assign the staff member working that slot.

06

Service visibility

Managers can see active sessions, waiter load, bill requests and basic service activity from one dashboard.

POS reconciliation

Your POS stays. The paper pad goes.

EasyBev does not need to replace the venue's point-of-sale system. The waiter simply opens the full item list at the POS and reconciles before producing the official bill.
